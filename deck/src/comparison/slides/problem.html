LITHUANIAN, 7 CASES

Foreign clubs stay in „…“ quotes and undeclined („Real“), while Lithuanian clubs decline: Kauno „Žalgiris“, „Žalgirio“.

LATVIAN, 7 CASES

Foreign names are respelled: Messi → Mesi, Ronaldo → Ronaldu. Keeping “Messi” is an error.

ESTONIAN, 14 CASES

Clubs have no quotes and decline like nouns: Reali vastu, Messist.

Names have to change form when they are the object of a verb, follow a preposition or follow a number. An instruction to keep names "as-is" guarantees broken grammar.
