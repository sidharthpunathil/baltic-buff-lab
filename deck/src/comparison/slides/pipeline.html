WHAT CHANGES FOR FANS AND OPERATORS

Every buff reaches the operator the way a local sports editor would write it

NAMES

Spelled like local press

Teams and players appear the way local sports press writes them.

GRAMMAR

Names fit the sentence

Every case form is prepared once per match, so a name never breaks the grammar.

SPORT TERMS

Fans hear their own sport

Local terms per sport, kept current by a linguist without an engineer.

WRITING

Written, not translated

Copy starts from the match facts in the fan’s language.

CHECKS

Obvious errors never reach review

Spelling, missing accents and literal translations are caught in milliseconds.

MEASUREMENT

Quality you can track

Every buff gets an error list and a score, per language.

REPAIR

Fixed before anyone sees it

Blocking errors are corrected automatically, at most twice.

OPERATOR

Approves with confidence

Native buff, its English meaning and open flags in one card.

Under the hood: Wikidata labels, case tables (Tēzaurs for Latvian), a style sheet, glossary and gold examples per language, deterministic validators, a blind MQM judge and a repair loop. The appendix lists the libraries.
