OUTCOME: A SECOND OPINION ON EVERY BUFF, IN SECONDS

Each buff is checked the way five different editors would check it

GRAMMAR

No broken cases, agreement or numerals.

SPORT TERMS

The words this country’s commentators use for this sport.

NAMES

The form the local press prints.

NATIVE EAR

Nothing that sounds translated or too formal.

FACTS

Score, leader and quiz answer match the match.

WHAT THE OPERATOR GETS

Only real problems hold a buff: checks that contradict the press, quote text that isn’t on the card or repeat another check are dropped. In round 2, 14 of 18 buffs needed no fix at all. All five checks run at once, in 2 to 4 seconds.

The checks never produce the score. An independent panel (a fresh Claude judge plus Gemini) scores every approach the same way.
