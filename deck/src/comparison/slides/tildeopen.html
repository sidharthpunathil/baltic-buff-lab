The test asks which of two near-identical sentences is grammatical. Choosing between our drafts is the same job.

WHY IT FITS

Its tokeniser treats Baltic text as fairly as English, and self-hosted open weights keep data in our own infrastructure.

WHY NOT AS THE WRITER

It is a base model: no instruction following or safety alignment yet. Tilde says a translation model built on it is next.

WHAT IT TAKES

About 61 GB of weights in bf16 (30.7B parameters): one 80 GB GPU for short prompts. Score 3 drafts per buff, keep the best.

Source: huggingface.co/TildeAI/TildeOpen-30b. Caveat from our own research: as a base model it ranked #119 of 217 on EuroEval Latvian, which tests instruction-style tasks, so it should rank drafts rather than write them.
