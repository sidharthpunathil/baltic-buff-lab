THE EXPERIMENT

Three approaches, judged blind on the same live moments

INPUT

3 simulated matches

Žalgiris vs Real Madrid (EuroLeague basketball), Latvia vs Canada (IIHF hockey), Estonia vs Italy (World Cup qualifier). 2 live moments each, real Wikidata entities.

OUTPUT

18 buffs per approach

6 moments × Lithuanian, Latvian, Estonian. Every buff is validated and judged the same way.

JUDGE

MQM, blind

Sees only the match facts and the card. Lists errors (minor, major, critical) and scores grammar, naturalness, terminology, facts and engagement.

ABLATION

First run: A and B on Sonnet 5.5 (this repo’s backend model), C on Opus 5.5. Second run: A and B on Opus 5.5 too, so the model and the pipeline can be separated.

The MQM method is what WMT uses for human and LLM evaluation of translation (GEMBA-MQM). An approved verdict means no major or critical errors.
