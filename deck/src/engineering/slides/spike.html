SPIKE PROPOSAL: TildeAI/TildeOpen-30b AS A GRAMMAR RANKER

Pick the most grammatical of three drafts with a model built for Baltic grammar

# 1. generate 3 candidate buffs (Claude, temperature varied)
drafts = [generate(moment) for _ in range(3)]

# 2. score each with TildeOpen log-probability
tok = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(
  "TildeAI/TildeOpen-30b", use_fast=False)
score = mean_token_logprob(model, tok, d.question)

# 3. keep the best that also passes validators
best = max(passing(drafts), key=score)

WHY

MultiBLiMP grammar test: 99.7% lt, 99.1% lv, 98.3% et, against 95.3 / 91.6 / 92.0% for Gemma 2 27b. Ranking drafts is the same task.

COST

30.7B params, about 61 GB in bf16: one 80 GB GPU, served with vLLM. Scoring needs one forward pass per draft, no generation.

LIMITS

Base model, CC-BY-4.0, no instruction tuning or safety alignment. Ranker only. Success bar: fewer grammar flags on the golden set at equal latency.

Model card: huggingface.co/TildeAI/TildeOpen-30b. The tokenizer must load with use_fast=False. Tilde says a translation model built on it is next, which would be worth re-testing as a writer.
