WHAT FANS SEE TODAY

Fans see their own players’ names in broken grammar

“Preserve numbers, team names, player names, and emoji as-is.”

JIL’s Translate button prompt in viewer_api_routes.py (translate_buff_content)

LITHUANIAN, 7 CASES

Foreign clubs stay in „…“ quotes and undeclined („Real“), while Lithuanian clubs decline: Kauno „Žalgiris“, „Žalgirio“.

LATVIAN, 7 CASES

Foreign names are respelled: Messi → Mesi, Ronaldo → Ronaldu. Keeping “Messi” is an error.

ESTONIAN, 14 CASES

Clubs have no quotes and decline like nouns: Reali vastu, Messist.

Names have to change form when they are the object of a verb, follow a preposition or follow a number. An instruction to keep names "as-is" guarantees broken grammar.

The translate step never sees the match, so it can’t catch a wrong fact. It is also told to keep names unchanged.

HOW IT’S USED

The generator gets the table and picks the case the sentence needs. The validator flags any name spelling that isn’t in the table.

WHERE FORMS COME FROM

Wikidata gives the nominative (lt “Kauno Žalgiris”, lv Kauņas “Žalgiris”). An LLM drafts the other cases under researched rules, Tēzaurs replaces the Latvian ones, and a native editor checks each table once.

Duolingo and Babbel never let free text decide a word form: they store a lemma and its paradigm. Sportradar does the same with typed name slots. One known miss: Latvian kept "Donnarumma" where Latvian press respells it, which is fixed by building the Latvian lemma from press usage before Tēzaurs runs.

THE EXPERIMENT

Three approaches, judged blind on the same live moments

INPUT

3 simulated matches

Žalgiris vs Real Madrid (EuroLeague basketball), Latvia vs Canada (IIHF hockey), Estonia vs Italy (World Cup qualifier). 2 live moments each, real Wikidata entities.

OUTPUT

18 buffs per approach

6 moments × Lithuanian, Latvian, Estonian. Every buff is validated and judged the same way.

JUDGE

MQM, blind

Sees only the match facts and the card. Lists errors (minor, major, critical) and scores grammar, naturalness, terminology, facts and engagement.

ABLATION

First run: A and B on Sonnet 5.5 (this repo’s backend model), C on Opus 5.5. Second run: A and B on Opus 5.5 too, so the model and the pipeline can be separated.

The MQM method is what WMT uses for human and LLM evaluation of translation (GEMBA-MQM). An approved verdict means no major or critical errors.

WHAT THE OPERATOR GETS

Only real problems hold a buff: checks that contradict the press, quote text that isn’t on the card or repeat another check are dropped. In round 2, 14 of 18 buffs needed no fix at all. All five checks run at once, in 2 to 4 seconds.

The checks never produce the score. An independent panel (a fresh Claude judge plus Gemini) scores every approach the same way.

TRY: TildeAI/TildeOpen-30b (CC-BY-4.0, open weights)

Built for our three languages: best used to pick the most grammatical draft

Grammar test (MultiBLiMP), from the model cardLithuanianLatvianEstonian
TildeOpen 1.1 30b99.7%99.1%98.3%
Gemma 2 27b95.3%91.6%92.0%

The test asks which of two near-identical sentences is grammatical. Choosing between our drafts is the same job.

WHY IT FITS

Its tokeniser treats Baltic text as fairly as English, and self-hosted open weights keep data in our own infrastructure.

WHY NOT AS THE WRITER

It is a base model: no instruction following or safety alignment yet. Tilde says a translation model built on it is next.

WHAT IT TAKES

About 61 GB of weights in bf16 (30.7B parameters): one 80 GB GPU for short prompts. Score 3 drafts per buff, keep the best.

Source: huggingface.co/TildeAI/TildeOpen-30b. Caveat from our own research: as a base model it ranked #119 of 217 on EuroEval Latvian, which tests instruction-style tasks, so it should rank drafts rather than write them.
