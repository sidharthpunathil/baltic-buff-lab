judge errors (4)
  • major terminology “Pēc divām trešdaļām”: A hockey period is 'periods', not 'trešdaļa'. The glossary explicitly rejects 'trešdaļa', and the card itself uses 'periodā' in the question, so the terminology is also inconsistent. → Pēc diviem periodiem
  • major accuracy-facts “Jā, noturēs! / Kanāda izlīdzinās / Latvija vēl iemetīs”: The options don't fit the yes/no question and overlap: if Latvija scores again, it has also kept its lead, so the first and third options are both true. The first option answers 'yes', but the others are not 'no' answers. Fans cannot choose clearly. → Turn it into an open question 'Kas notiks trešajā periodā?' with mutually exclusive outcomes.
  • minor grammar-other “Latvija vēl iemetīs”: The verb 'iemest' has no object. In polished copy it should be 'iemetīs vārtus'. → Latvija iemetīs vēl vārtus
  • minor fluency-translationese “noturēs 2:1 vadību”: Putting the score before the noun reads like a calque of English '2:1 lead'. Latvian editors would write 'nosargās 2:1' or 'noturēs vadību'. → nosargās 2:1
